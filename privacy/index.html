Privacy Policy — Ringwall: Spam Call Blocker

Effective date: October 26, 2026

Ringwall: Spam Call Blocker ("the App") is published by CauGiay Hotel ("we", "us", "our"). The App provides caller ID, spam-number warnings, call blocking, a phone dialer / in-call screen, a contacts and call-history view, and an "unseen messages" view for supported messaging apps. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App accesses, how it is used, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. It applies to the Android app with package name com.caugiay.caller.id.spam.scanner.

If you do not agree with this Policy, please do not use the App.

1. Summary

  • Your contacts, call history, blocked numbers and message previews are processed on your device. The App does not upload them to a server operated by us.
  • Spam lookups use a database bundled inside the App, so a phone number is not sent anywhere to be looked up.
  • The App is free and supported by advertising. Our advertising, attribution, analytics and crash-reporting partners (listed in Section 5) collect device and usage data such as your advertising ID, IP address and app events.
  • The App does not record phone-call audio, and it does not require an account.

The App asks for each permission only for the purpose described. You can decline or revoke permissions in Android Settings; some features then stop working.

3. Spam database and caller ID

Caller ID and spam warnings are produced on your device by matching a phone number against (a) your own contacts and block list, (b) a spam-number database that is bundled with the App, and (c) simple on-device heuristics. The bundled database is derived from the open-source CallShield community dataset (MIT License), which aggregates publicly available complaint data (for example FTC/FCC complaints, PhoneBlock and community reports). Spam labels are informational and may be inaccurate or outdated. The phone numbers you look up are not transmitted to us.

6. Data retention and deletion
